Why I do this

So, let me say this first. I love clothes that carry a story. A coat that survived three owners. A dress somebody saved for Sunday. Fast fashion can't give you that.

Margin Material started with thrifting. I learned to read a garment the way you read a letter. The more I found, the more I wanted to style, and the more I styled, the more I wanted to make.

That's how one passion became three chapters. I style. I find. I make. And I document all of it so you can watch a piece travel from the bin or the sketchbook to your closet.
