The story

Why I do this.

So, let me say this first. I love clothes that carry a story. A coat that survived three owners. A dress somebody saved for Sunday. Fast fashion can't give you that.

Margin Material started with thrifting. I learned to read a garment the way you read a letter: the seams, the fabric weight, the hardware. The more I found, the more I wanted to make, and custom dresses followed.

Today I curate vintage, build custom pieces to order and document the whole process so you can watch a piece go from the bin (or the sketchbook) to your closet.

I write too. Storytelling shapes how I see every garment, and it's why each listing here comes with the story behind it.

"Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart." Colossians 3:23 — the standard behind every seam
