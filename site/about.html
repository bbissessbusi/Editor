The story

Why I do this.

So, let me say this first. I love clothes that carry a story. A coat that survived three owners. A dress somebody saved for Sunday. Fast fashion can't give you that.

I learned to read a garment the way you read a letter: the seams, the fabric weight, the hardware. The more I found, the more I wanted to make. Custom dresses followed, and then friends started asking me to dress them too.

Margin Material holds all of it. I style women for the life they live, release vintage in drops and build custom pieces to order. I document the whole process so you can watch a piece go from the bin (or the sketchbook) to your closet.

I write too. Storytelling shapes how I see every garment, and it's why each piece here comes with the story behind it.
