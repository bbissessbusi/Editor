Pull your Depop inventory

Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar:

Pull Depop inventory

  1. Open depop.com/margin_material_vintage on your computer.
  2. Click the Pull Depop inventory bookmark.
  3. Wait while it scrolls through your shop. It saves depop-feed.json to your Downloads.
  4. Send that file to Claude, or put it in concepts/journal/assets/. The shop updates from it.

Works in Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox on a computer. Nothing is sent anywhere: the file is made in your browser and saved to your computer.
